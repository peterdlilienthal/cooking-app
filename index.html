📋 Assumptions
  • PV output is taken from PVGIS at the optimal fixed tilt for the location (equator-facing), including its cell-temperature and 14% system-loss model.
  • The battery is controlled to have a minimum state of charge of 10%, and a round trip efficiency of 95%.
  • The demand is spread evenly within the 2 demand periods.
